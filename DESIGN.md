## Brand & Style

The brand serves as Thailand’s premier VIP Van and curated mobility marketplace. It bridges the gap between leisure travelers seeking dependable private charters and enterprise organizers requiring certified fleets, transparent pricing, and official tax documentation.

### Core Character & Personality
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Reassuring, legitimate, verified drivers, official yellow-plate standards (ป้ายเหลือง 30), zero hidden booking fees.
- **Effortless & Direct:** Frictionless direct-to-driver booking via phone and LINE messaging without middleman friction.
- **Premium Utility:** A clean aesthetic combining the refined elegance of luxury hospitality marketplaces with the functional precision of high-velocity mobility consoles.

### Visual Style
The aesthetic sits squarely in **Modern Clean Utility Marketplace** (a fusion of minimalist hospitality directories and high-density mobility apps). It relies on pristine slate canvases, razor-sharp hairline borders, subtle multi-stop elevation shadows, micro-pill status badges, and crystal-clear Thai-Latin typographic hierarchy.

## Colors

The color palette reinforces reliability, verified public transport compliance, and frictionless user action:

- **Primary (`#0B2545` & `#133E87`):** Deep Navy represents institutional trust, high-end VIP transport, and corporate travel reliability.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`):** High-clarity Mobility Blue is used for primary navigational taps, active filter toggles, search submission buttons, and links.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Warm Amber commands visual interest for ratings, highlighted specials, and review metrics.
- **Direct-to-Driver Actions:**
  - Phone actions utilize sapphire-to-navy tones (`#2563EB`).
  - Instant chat actions utilize the authentic LINE green (`#06C755`) to signal immediate communication channels.
- **Verification Indicators:**
  - Transport License Yellow (`#EAB308` / `#FEFCE8`) signifies official commercial yellow-plate vehicle certification (ป้ายเหลือง 30).
  - Emerald Green (`#10B981` / `#ECFDF5`) designates verified driver background checks and identity verification.
- **Neutral & Canvas:** Crisp Slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`) forms the base layer, keeping cards (`#FFFFFF`) distinct with hairline slate borders (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes bilingual harmony across Thai and Latin scripts. **Nunito Sans** pairs with system fallback stacks (`Prompt`, `Sarabun`, `Noto Sans Thai`) to maintain optical balance, open counter spaces, and legibility at compact badge sizes.

### Usage Standards
- **Headlines & Value Propositions:** Heavy weights (700 to 800) with slight negative tracking create an intentional, premium magazine feel.
- **Numbers & Pricing:** Display price figures (`฿2,400 / วัน`) use heavy weight styling with tabular numeric alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for clean vertical scanning across vehicle cards.
- **Badges & Trust Seals:** Tight, bold uppercase or compact Thai wording with slight positive tracking ensures legibility at 10–12px heights.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column adaptive fluid grid engineered for quick evaluation, side-by-side spec comparison, and clear workflow steps:

### Breakpoints & Enclosures
- **Desktop (`1280px+`):** 12-column grid, max content width `1200px` centered, `20px` gutters, outer section margin `24px` to `32px`.
- **Tablet (`768px - 1024px`):** 6-column or 8-column grid with `16px` gutters. Comparison grids collapse cleanly into 2 columns.
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):** Single column fluid canvas, `16px` margins. Action buttons within vehicle cards sit side-by-side in dual 50/50 layouts for one-thumb reachability.

### Spacing Rhythm
- Compact internal component padding (`8px` to `12px`) keeps data density high, allowing users to compare van models without excessive scrolling.
- Section intervals step by `2rem` (`32px`) to `3rem` (`48px`) to segment search, search listings, live TripBoard, and corporate quote modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through hairline borders combined with soft multi-layer ambient shadows rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Surface color `#F8FAFC` without shadow. Used for page canvas and nested segmented control wells.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, ambient shadow `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Card Hover / Interactive Lift):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, shadow `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`, translateY `-2px`.
- **Level 3 (Search Console & Sticky Header):** Surface `#FFFFFF` with glassmorphic backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md bg-white/95`), border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`, shadow `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Bottom Sheet Driver Dossier):** Shadow `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.18)`, overlaid on backdrop scrim `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.55)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances contemporary consumer app warmth with professional structure:

- **Cards & Enclosures:** `14px` border radius provides a refined, friendly card frame that separates vehicle photos cleanly without clipping important vehicle details.
- **Interactive Inputs & Buttons:** `10px` border radius ensures form fields, select triggers, and CTA buttons feel tactile and precise.
- **Pills & Status Badges:** `9999px` (full pill) for category filters, passenger seat capacity labels, verified driver badges, and yellow-plate indicators.
- **Modals & Dialogs:** `20px` to match modern mobile sheet conventions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Search / Booking CTA:** Solid Mobility Blue (`#2563EB`), hover `#1D4ED8`, text white, `10px` radius, font weight 700. Features active scale effect (`scale-[0.98]`).
- **Direct-to-Driver Phone CTA:** Deep blue tone (`#0B2545` or `#2563EB`), phone handset icon prefix, tabular phone preview text (`089-xxx-4321`), weight 700.
- **Direct-to-Driver LINE CTA:** Vibrant LINE Green (`#06C755`), hover `#05B04B`, chat bubble icon prefix, text white, weight 700.
- **Secondary / Filter Reset:** Ghost or outlined button with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, slate text `#475569`, hover `#F1F5F9`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Yellow 30 Plate (ป้ายเหลือง 30):** Amber-yellow fill (`#FEFCE8`), text `#854D0E`, border `1px solid #FEF08A`, bold badge typography with a vehicle shield icon.
- **Driver Verified:** Emerald fill (`#ECFDF5`), text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`, checkmark badge icon.
- **Feature Specs:** Slate fill (`#F1F5F9`), text `#334155`, subtle text label highlighting amenities (Captain Seats, Android TV, YouTube, Free Wi-Fi).
- **Seat Capacity Pill:** High-contrast dark badge placed directly on the vehicle image overlay (e.g. `9 ที่นั่ง`, `13 ที่นั่ง`) with semi-transparent navy background (`bg-slate-900/80` text white).

### Vehicle & Offer Cards
- Clean vertical card divided into:
  1. **Media Area:** High-resolution 16:9 vehicle exterior or interior photo, badge tags pinned top-left (seats) and top-right (verified status).
  2. **Identity & Driver Info:** Driver thumbnail with verified ring, rating score with golden star, vehicle name/model title in bold Slate-900.
  3. **Amenity Grid:** Inline pill row displaying key features (Captain Seats, Electric Step, Smart TV).
  4. **Commercial Guarantee:** Subtle 1-line strip confirming official commercial insurance and zero booking markup.
  5. **Price & Action Footer:** Clear daily base rate (`฿2,400 / วัน`), followed by dual side-by-side action buttons (`โทรเลย` and `ทัก LINE`).

### TripBoard & Matching Feed Rows
- Horizontal list cards containing route origin/destination tags, requested trip date, passenger count, customer budget preview, and direct response triggers for active drivers.

### Search Console & Filter Bar
- Unified elevated container anchored below the hero banner. Includes integrated vehicle segment tabs (VIP Vans, Commuter 10-14, Luxury Alphard/Vellfire, SUV/Cars), departure location picker, date picker, seat quantity dropdown, and primary "ค้นหารถ" CTA.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Select Fields:** Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, focus ring `2px solid #2563EB`.
- **Segmented Control Tabs:** Pill container in `#F1F5F9` with active tab animated into an elevated white pill (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle shadow and navy text.
